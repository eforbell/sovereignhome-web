Subscription Fatigue

$12/month here, $15/month there. Budgeting apps, calendar tools, recipe organizers — your family's digital basics cost hundreds a year and you own nothing.

Data You Don't Control

Your budget lives on someone else's server. Your recipes are in someone else's database. If the company changes terms or shuts down, your data goes with it.

One-Size-Fits-None

Every family is different, but SaaS apps are designed for the average of everyone. You can't customize what you don't own.

How It Works

Four steps from subscription renter to sovereign homeowner.

Get a Server

An old laptop, a Raspberry Pi, or a small mini PC. Anything that runs Linux.

Install Home Base

One command sets up your control plane. It handles everything else from there.

Choose Your Apps

Pick what your family needs. Install them with a click. Skip what you don't.

You're Sovereign

Your data stays home. Your apps run locally. Customize, extend, and grow at your pace.

Why This Exists

Software should serve families, not extract from them.

Your Data, Your Home

No cloud syncing to servers you don't control. No terms of service that change without notice. Your family's information lives on your hardware, under your roof, end of story.

Open Source, Open Standard

Every line of code is public. Every app uses open web standards — HTML, CSS, JavaScript. No proprietary lock-in, no app store gatekeepers. Fork it, fix it, make it yours.

Bitcoin Native

Sovereign money is part of a sovereign digital life. Bitcoin Accounting gives families proper treasury management — cost basis tracking, multi-wallet verification, and tax reporting — without handing data to a third party.

Grow Your Own

We provide the seedling — working software that handles the basics well. You grow it into whatever your family needs. Add features, change designs, integrate what matters to you. It's yours to cultivate.
